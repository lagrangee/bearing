# Code of Conduct

Bearing uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to [lagrangee@gmail.com](mailto:lagrangee@gmail.com). Do not submit a conduct report through a public Issue or Discussion. If a report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's platform-level [abuse reporting path](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
