Project Brief

At a Glance

Northstar gives product teams a dependable release workspace for customer-facing SaaS changes.

Current Position

Public Beta is close. The release candidate is ready for review, but the Gate has not passed. One Planning Review needs human judgment before that decision.

Established Baseline

  • Workspace isolation and audit export are in the accepted product baseline.

  • Customer-facing changes remain read-only until an owner accepts the decision.
