# Effort Owner

## Applicability

Use for Effort creation, scope, planned/active/concluded lifecycle, or Work Binding creation,
change, or removal.

## Authority

Work Binding is owned by the Effort owner because it is part of the Effort's semantic commitment to
one provider-native scope. Provider Modules validate identity and acquisition facts; they do not
choose the relation. Native lifecycle remains with Work Management.

## Operation

1. Inspect the complete Effort reference, owning Roadmap, Target Gate, current Binding, direct
   Authorities, current native facts, and diagnostics. Establish one complete scope and lifecycle
   baseline. For conclusion, complete the Conclusion discovery below before drafting a candidate.
2. Author one exact candidate for planned creation, activation, conclusion, or Binding change.
   Defer a bare-Matt enrollment candidate until Native Work has completed standalone and direct
   relationship evidence exists. Provider completion, native lifecycle, Gate readiness, and
   Passage remain evidence rather than transition authority. Concluded Efforts never reopen
   implicitly. Conclusion uses its dedicated sequence below; other non-Matt operations continue
   through the canonical contract without the Matt steps below.
3. For a request that starts one exact planned Effort, confirm the planned
   Effort, selected Provider Configuration, intended native workflow, and direct authorization
   before native work starts. State the later combined Binding and Activation effect visibly. The
   unambiguous request supplies its Accepted Decision; do not ask for duplicate confirmation.
4. Let the selected Work Management owner create or identify the real provider-native scope.
   Preserve an explicitly user-invoked Matt Skill; otherwise use Ordinary Owner Work without
   claiming Skill use. The Effort owner does not predict successful native subjects, fabricate
   provider identity, or author Work Management meaning.
5. As soon as native scope creation or identification completes, preserve its exact provider-canonical
   identity and re-read current Bearing State and every candidate precondition. Do this before
   implementation, without waiting for the whole delivery or Map resolution. Concurrent canonical
   change invalidates the candidate and stops without
   merge. With unchanged valid inputs, one canonical Planning Transaction creates the Work Binding,
   changes the Effort from `planned` to `active`, and records its Activation Bearing Event Commit
   Time at this accepted activation. Publish no intermediate planned-and-bound state. A valid unchanged candidate that cannot
   apply is a Bearing contract or implementation defect, not authority for retry, repair, or
   fallback.
6. For a bare Matt invocation, do not enter the named-start path or condition native handling on
   Bearing enrollment. Native Work first completes the full owner operation as Standalone Native
   Work. Only then may its bounded advisory result enter the Consent Outcomes below. The Enrollment
   Suggestion is transient Agent guidance: add no persisted enrollment workflow, dispatcher,
   session mode, or Matt-owned contract state.
7. For explicit acceptance, re-read every current canonical input and author one complete candidate
   for the exact standalone scope. Apply the matching Consent Outcome in one canonical Planning
   Transaction, then apply the required baseline boundary below.

## Conclusion

1. Inspect the Effort's **Owned Assets** relation. Require `confirmed-none`, or `present` with
   complete coverage and available targets. For every owned Asset, inspect its current Disposition
   and its own **Authority Baselines** reverse relation with the same completeness requirement.
   Direct Effort Authorities are not the complete set of adopters. Unknown, unavailable, partial,
   or at-least coverage leaves discovery incomplete; resolve the evidence gap before presenting
   a complete conclusion candidate. Completion: every owned Asset and every current adopter is
   accounted for, including Authorities absent from the Effort's list.
2. Separate active Assets from historical archived or superseded Assets. Historical Assets may
   retain this Effort, their Source, and all existing event times. With no active Owned Assets,
   keep the conclusion scope minimal. For each active Asset, inspect its actual continuing use
   and propose a reasoned transfer to an existing eligible owner, valid supersession, or archival.
   A transfer destination has continuing responsibility; an Effort destination is not concluded.
   Retain source material. Asset disposition is a Human decision, not automatic archival, deletion,
   registration, adoption, forced Authority ownership, or creation of a holding Effort.
3. Use the selected Asset and Authority owners to compose the effects. Owner transfer preserves
   Asset identity and Source and leaves Baseline membership unchanged. Superseding or archiving
   an adopted Asset requires replacement or removal in every affected Authority's Baseline.
   Resolve each replacement and verify it remains active and does not form a replacement cycle.
   Completion: all necessary Asset effects and exact Baseline before/after memberships form one
   legal candidate whose concluded Effort owns no active Asset.
4. Present that candidate with Effort disposition and rationale, each Asset's destination or
   terminal disposition and reason, every Baseline change, and the actual owner file write set.
   Reading destination eligibility does not require changing that destination's file. Preserve
   the existing Work Binding, historical events, and independent Gate truth. Existing acceptance
   covering every unchanged effect is sufficient; present newly discovered material effects for
   acceptance first. Refusal or partial acceptance withholds the remaining effects and cannot
   authorize a conclusion that would still violate its complete preconditions.
5. Immediately before writing, re-read affected files and incoming relations against the accepted
   candidate, including Owned Assets, every affected Authority Baseline, destination eligibility,
   and replacement state. A newly owned Asset, changed membership, or newly inactive replacement
   invalidates the candidate. Preserve others' changes and return the material difference for a
   new decision instead of silently merging it.
6. Apply the complete accepted owner write set through the canonical contract, including its
   post-edit validation and partial-write recovery. Record the actual conclusion and any Asset
   terminal event times; if incomplete, report the exact remaining conclusion effects. Completion:
   accepted source state and the committed readback agree, with zero new structural defects and
   no active Asset owned by the concluded Effort.

## Consent Outcomes

| Decision | Target condition | Canonical outcome | Provider follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `refuse` | `standalone-scope; existing-planned-effort` | `no-binding; planned-not-created; no-lifecycle-event; no-hidden-recommendation-state` | `none` |
| `accept-existing` | `standalone-scope; planned; no-binding` | `binding; active; activation-event-time` | `exact-scope-capture` |
| `accept-new` | `standalone-scope; useful-new-effort` | `complete-commitment; binding; active; activation-event-time` | `exact-scope-capture` |
| `accept-existing-bound` | `binding-exists` | `reject; no-winning-scope; no-rebind; no-duplicate-effort` | `none` |

## After this operation

- **Required:** A new or changed Work Binding enters the selected Project Read Model operation
  after the canonical contract's affected-target and diagnostics readback is complete. Use the
  exact scope from that validated current Binding as the baseline acquisition input before
  baseline-dependent work. Once capture completes successfully, recheck the current Binding and ready basis and
  continue the still-authorized owner request. This internal boundary requires no extra Human
  reply. Failed, partial, or unknown acquisition preserves the accepted Binding and active Effort
  while stopping dependent work at Native Work's failure boundary.
- **Required:** Wayfinder does not activate the Effort before its Map scope exists and does not wait
  for Map resolution. Ordinary bug and enhancement workflows use the same owner order and lifecycle
  boundary.
- **Required:** After an accepted Effort conclusion, root selects the Project Brief owner for the
  bounded terminal-orientation evaluation: refresh materially stale orientation or explicitly
  report a current Brief as `no-op`. Project Summary participates only for accepted long-horizon
  meaning or its existing owner trigger. Creation and activation do not select Brief evaluation.
- **Consider:** Recommend Wayfinder when discovery would help, but keep it optional and effect-free;
  an ordinary bug or enhancement workflow remains usable.
- **Do not infer:** Native artifacts, status, tests, provider completion, capture, reconciliation,
  Portal observation, or diagnostics enrolls, activates, or concludes an Effort, passes a Gate,
  completes a Roadmap, or establishes release readiness.

## Completion criterion

The accepted Effort scope and lifecycle are current, Work Binding stayed with this owner, and every
new or changed Binding has a truthful exact-scope baseline result. A conclusion also has complete
accepted Asset and Authority effects, valid affected-target readback, and a Brief evaluation.

## Write form

For a new record write `.bearing/state/efforts/<slug>.md`. Required frontmatter is `Type: effort`,
`ID: effort:<slug>`, plain-text `Title`, `Roadmap`, `Target gate`, unique `Authorities`, `Citations`,
`Lifecycle`, and `Planned at`. Both collections are required even when `[]`. Authorities contains
Authority Stable IDs; Citations follows the shared form. The Target gate belongs to this Roadmap
and includes this Effort in its complete Effort order. Required `## Intent` is nonempty free
Markdown; required `## Work` is free Markdown and may be empty. Work prose or links do not create a
Binding or substitute for its fields.

| Lifecycle | Applicable fields |
| --- | --- |
| `planned` | Planned at; omit Work binding, Activated at, and Conclusion. |
| `active` | Preserve Planned at; require Activated at and exactly one Work binding; omit Conclusion. |
| `concluded` | Preserve Planned at, Activated at, and Work binding; require Conclusion. |

`Work binding` is one mapping with exactly `Provider: matt-skills/v1` and `Native scope` copied from
the real provider-canonical scope established by Work Management. It is a normalized Local scope
locator or the Provider's exact opaque GitHub scope. Obtain identity through the Operation
above; the Local locator in an example is not evidence that such native work exists.

`Conclusion` requires `Disposition` (`completed`, `withdrawn`, or `superseded`), plain-text
`Rationale`, and `Concluded at`. Only `superseded` requires `Replacement effort: effort:<slug>`;
the other dispositions omit that field. Its target must be an existing different Effort in the
same Roadmap and with the same Target gate, without a replacement cycle. An Effort concluding
cannot retain active Owned Assets; historical inactive Assets can retain it as owner. Gate Passage
and native completion stay independent.

New accepted events record their own UTC instants. Existing Planned at, Activated at, or Concluded
at may be null when historical time is unavailable; their required keys still apply. The decoder
can read an existing `.scratch/<slug>/effort.md` locator and expose historical Binding defects as
diagnostics. Preserve these records for scoped decisions; neither a legacy locator nor a decoded
planned-and-bound or active-without-Binding record is the canonical new write form.

### Complete planned example

```markdown .bearing/state/efforts/save-notes.md
---
Type: effort
ID: effort:save-notes
Title: Save and reopen a note
Roadmap: roadmap:local-notes
Target gate: gate:notes-persist
Authorities: []
Citations: []
Lifecycle: planned
Planned at: 2026-09-01T09:00:00.000Z
---

# Save and reopen a note

## Intent

Allow a person to save a note and reopen its original content locally.

## Work

Native work has not started.
```

### Complete active alternative

This shape is valid only after Work Management has actually established the shown native scope
and the accepted activation records both facts together. It does not prove baseline acquisition.

```markdown .bearing/state/efforts/save-notes.md
---
Type: effort
ID: effort:save-notes
Title: Save and reopen a note
Roadmap: roadmap:local-notes
Target gate: gate:notes-persist
Authorities: []
Citations: []
Lifecycle: active
Planned at: 2026-09-01T09:00:00.000Z
Activated at: 2026-09-01T10:00:00.000Z
Work binding:
  Provider: matt-skills/v1
  Native scope: .scratch/save-notes
---

# Save and reopen a note

## Intent

Allow a person to save a note and reopen its original content locally.

## Work

The specification lives in the actual save-notes native scope. Delivery is underway.
```

necessary Asset effects. The fictional completion says nothing about Gate Passage.
